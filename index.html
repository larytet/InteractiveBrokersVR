InteractiveBrokersVR

An unofficial design mock of a brokerage mobile app in VR. It is not affiliated with Interactive Brokers, uses none of its logos or assets, and shows example data only. No order is sent anywhere and nothing asks for a login. Drag the background to look around, click to press, hold the blue Submit button to place a paper order. With a headset browser press Enter VR and use the controller ray and trigger.

Screens

  • Left, the watchlist. Center, the chart with periods, or the portfolio, orders, markets, news and menu, chosen by the tab bar at the bottom of the board. Right, the order ticket.
  • The top bar holds the account figures.

Portfolio constellation

  • On the table, every watchlist symbol is an orb orbiting the account core. Held positions are bigger, green or red by the day change; the core and its beam follow the day P&L.
  • Press an orb to select the symbol, which updates the chart and the ticket. Mouse wheel, thumbstick up and down, or the deck buttons zoom in and out, and the last deck button changes what the size means.
  • Submitting a paper order sends a pulse through the orb.

Sky

  • Look up for the indices ring and the news ring. Both are example data on a timer.

Input

  • One ray and one trigger per hand, large targets, no sliders: values change with plus and minus buttons.
  • Submitting needs a hold of 1.2 s; releasing early cancels.
  • Drag the bar at the top of a panel to move it; the right mouse button or the controller grip drags a panel from anywhere. Reset layout is in the Menu tab.
